I ussed the Vite bundling framework in my portfolio and configured it as a multi-page build. The build combines and minifies my JavaScript modules and CSS into hashed files in the dist/ folder, while the service worker, manifest, and images are kept in public/ so they keep their exact filenames. Lastly, I deployed the bundled application to Render, where Express serves the dist/ folder in production.
